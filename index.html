1. Overview

"Adwiaty & Mawidi – Personal Organizer" is a productivity and organizational tool designed to help users manage their daily schedules, appointments, and personal reminders. This application is intended for individual use as a personal assistant tool.

2. Data Collection and Storage

  • Offline Operation: The app operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal, medical, or sensitive information on external servers.
  • Local Storage: All data entered (titles, times, notes) is stored locally on your device's memory. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app will result in the permanent deletion of this local data.

3. Camera and Media Permissions

  • Camera Access: The app requests camera access to allow users to take photos as "Visual References" for their appointments (e.g., photographing labels or reminders).
  • Photo Library: The app accesses the gallery to allow users to attach existing images to their scheduled tasks for better organization.
  • Data Handling: Images captured or selected are processed and stored locally only. We have no access to your photos, and they are never uploaded to the cloud.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to outside parties. Since no data is collected, there is no data to share.

5. Medical Disclaimer

This app is strictly a scheduling and organizational tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users should not rely on this app for critical medical decisions. Always consult with a professional healthcare provider.
